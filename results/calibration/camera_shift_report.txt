Verdict
   The calibration's own validated error is -0.81 % on a 1.48 m reference,
   which is 12 mm (results/calibration/homography_validation_report.txt).
   The largest camera displacement measured here is 8.4 mm.

   The camera moved less than the calibration's own measurement uncertainty.
   Reusing the calibration across these recordings is therefore justified,
   as a measured bound rather than as an assumption.

Limit of this check
   An affine warp captures translation, rotation, scale and shear. It does
   not capture a change in the camera's distance from, or angle to, the
   ground plane, which would alter perspective rather than the image
   similarity. A near-zero result here means the two views are close in
   image terms; it is strong evidence for, but not proof of, an unmoved camera.
